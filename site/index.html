How far is the metro? Walking access to STM stations in Montréal

Many students and residents in Montreal rely on the metro every day, but a station that looks close on a map can be a long or difficult walk. This map helps them see which areas have good walking access to an STM metro station and which do not.

This map is for

Students and residents in Montreal who want to check how easy it is to walk to the metro.

What you can do here
